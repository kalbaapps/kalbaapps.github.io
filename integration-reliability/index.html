Integration Reliability

Know when your systems quietly disagree.

A tool that runs small business-style checks across your systems (for example "an employee created in HR must reach Payroll with the same salary"), tells you in plain English what went wrong, and alerts you by email, chat or phone.

  • User guide (set up your server, connect the app, add checks)
  • Privacy policy (Android app)
  • Request the server download, ask a question or report a problem
